    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How fast can a technician reach me?

    Our average response time is under 2 hours for most areas in Kochi. In peak summer (March–May) it may go up to 3 hours. After booking, you receive the technician's name and live tracking via WhatsApp.

    Is there a warranty on the repair?

    All repairs come with a 90-day service warranty. If the same issue recurs within that period, we fix it at no additional cost. Spare parts also carry the manufacturer's warranty on top of ours.

    Do you use genuine spare parts?

    Yes, always. We source only OEM-approved spare parts and never use duplicates or low-quality components, so your appliance performs reliably for years after every repair.

    How much does a visit cost?

    There is a nominal visit/inspection charge of ₹299, which is fully waived if you proceed with the repair. Before any work begins, our technician gives you a complete cost breakdown — you only pay for what you approve.

    Which brands do you work on?

    All major brands — Daikin, LG, Samsung, Voltas, Blue Star, Carrier, Hitachi, Whirlpool, Godrej, Panasonic, Haier, O General, Mitsubishi, IFB, Bosch and more. Both older and latest models.

    What is an AMC, and is it worth it?

    An AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) is a yearly plan covering scheduled visits, priority support and repair discounts. Customers typically save 40–60% compared to pay-per-service pricing, and it significantly extends appliance lifespan.

    What are your working hours?

    We take bookings every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, including Sundays and public holidays. Our emergency helpline stays open 24/7 for urgent breakdowns. We recommend booking early on Sundays as slots fill up quickly.

    How often should I service my AC?

    At least twice a year — before and after summer (March and October). For heavily used or dusty environments, quarterly servicing is advisable. Regular servicing improves efficiency and lowers electricity bills.

    Do you handle commercial / VRV systems?

    Absolutely — we specialise in VRV/VRF multi-split systems, cassette ACs and central cooling for offices, hotels, hospitals and malls. We offer customised AMC plans for businesses with a dedicated account manager.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, debit/credit cards and bank transfers. Payment is made only after the work is completed to your satisfaction, and you get a digital invoice on WhatsApp/email.
